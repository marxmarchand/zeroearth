**Subject:** Licence question: SAGE Global Potential Vegetation dataset (5 arc-minute) on a non-commercial website

Dear Professor Ramankutty,

I run zeroearth ([URL]), a non-commercial website that shows Earth-system data on a 3D globe. The site has no advertising, no paywall and no user accounts. Every layer states its source, licence, time reference and how the data were derived.

We would like to show the Global Potential Vegetation dataset (Ramankutty & Foley 1999) as a reference layer, next to observed land cover. The legend would describe it as your paper defines it: vegetation in equilibrium with present-day climate in the absence of human activities, not a past state. It would also say that it combines DISCover remnant vegetation with the Haxeltine & Prentice biome model.

The SAGE download page (sage.nelson.wisc.edu) offers the 5 arc-minute and 0.5° files but does not state a licence or terms of reuse. Before publishing anything, I would like to ask:

1. **Licence:** Under which licence or terms may the 5 arc-minute dataset be reused?
2. **Our planned use:**
   - We would store a derived file in the website's public repository: the 15 classes, possibly reprojected or tiled, with a full citation.
   - The browser would load it from there to draw the map.
   - The file is not offered as a separate download, but anyone could technically retrieve it.
   - Would that be acceptable?
3. **Further sources:** Is there a newer version, or a version with a provenance layer that shows which cells come from satellite data and which from the biome model?

For the 0.5° version we can use the ISLSCP II copy at the ORNL DAAC. The question concerns the finer 5 arc-minute grid.

Thank you for your time and for making these data available.

Kind regards,
